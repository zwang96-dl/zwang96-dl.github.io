Privacy Policy — Tech News

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Tech News is a tech news reader app. This policy explains what data the app handles — in short, we collect nothing.

1. Data collection

  • Tech News does not collect any personal data.
  • No user accounts, no sign-in, no profiles.
  • No analytics, no usage statistics, no crash reporting sent to the developer.
  • No advertising and no third-party trackers of any kind.

2. DeepSeek API key

The app's optional translation and Chinese digest features use the DeepSeek API with a key that you provide yourself in the app's Settings page.

  • Your key is stored only on your own device — in the iOS Keychain (via Expo SecureStore) on iPhone, and in your browser's localStorage on web.
  • The key is never sent to the developer, never uploaded to any server we operate, and never included in any code repository.
  • When you tap translate or Chinese digest, your device connects directly to the DeepSeek API (api.deepseek.com) with your key. The request goes only between your device and DeepSeek, subject to DeepSeek's own privacy policy.
  • You can delete your key at any time from the Settings page; doing so removes it from your device.

3. News content

The app fetches publicly available news feeds (RSS/Atom/JSON) from third-party publishers you choose to follow. Reading happens in the app or in your browser on the publisher's own site, governed by each publisher's privacy policy.

4. Children

The app is a general-audience news reader and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same URL with an updated date.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue at github.com/zwang96-dl/tech-news-app/issues.

隐私政策 — Tech News（科技新闻）

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 14 日

Tech News 是一款科技新闻阅读 App。本政策说明 App 如何处理数据——一句话：我们不收集任何数据。

1. 数据收集

  • Tech News 不收集任何个人数据。
  • 无账号、无登录、无用户画像。
  • 无统计分析、无用量上报，不向开发者发送任何崩溃报告。
  • 无广告、无任何第三方追踪。

2. DeepSeek API Key

App 的翻译和中文速读功能使用 DeepSeek API，Key 由您自己在 App 的设置页输入。

  • 您的 Key 只保存在您自己的设备上——iPhone 上存于系统钥匙串（经由 Expo SecureStore），网页版存于浏览器 localStorage。
  • Key 永远不会发送给开发者，不会上传到我们运营的任何服务器，也不会出现在任何代码仓库里。
  • 当您点击翻译或中文速读时，您的设备会直接连接 DeepSeek API（api.deepseek.com）。请求只在您的设备与 DeepSeek 之间传输，适用 DeepSeek 自己的隐私政策。
  • 您可以随时在设置页删除 Key，删除即从设备上清除。

3. 新闻内容

App 从您关注的第三方媒体抓取公开的新闻源（RSS/Atom/JSON）。阅读发生在 App 内或媒体自己的网站上，适用各媒体自己的隐私政策。

4. 儿童

本 App 是面向大众的新闻阅读器，不收集任何人的数据，包括 13 岁以下儿童。

5. 政策变更

本政策如有变更，将发布在本页面并更新日期。

6. 联系方式

对本政策有疑问，请前往 github.com/zwang96-dl/tech-news-app/issues 提交 issue。
